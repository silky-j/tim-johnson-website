About Tim

He will never call attention to himself — so his kids built this page to do it for him. Here is the short version of a long and generous career in survey research.

Timothy P. Johnson is a survey methodologist with more than thirty years of experience studying measurement and nonresponse error. His research centers on a deceptively hard problem: the cultural variability in how people cognitively process and answer survey questions — and what that means for the data we build policy and science on.

From 1996 to 2019 he directed the Survey Research Laboratory at the University of Illinois Chicago, where he generated nearly $30 million in research funding over his tenure. His work spans health behaviors among disadvantaged populations and a broad range of public-health topics, always with an eye toward making measurement more rigorous and more equitable.

Today he serves as a Senior Research Fellow at NORC at the University of Chicago and as Professor Emeritus at the University of Illinois Chicago. Among his many recognitions: the 2023 AAPOR Exceptionally Distinguished Achievement Award — the association’s highest lifetime honor — Fellow of the American Statistical Association (2015), the AAPOR Book Award (2013), and service as president of AAPOR, WAPOR, MAPOR, and AASRO.

He is, by every available measure, far too modest about all of it.
