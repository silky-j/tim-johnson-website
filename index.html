About Tim

A career spent listening carefully

Tim has spent his career on a deceptively simple question: how do we ask better questions - and get answers we can trust - across very different communities and cultures?

Read the full story

  • Cross-cultural survey pioneer - advancing how we measure response behavior across cultures and languages.
  • World-renowned methodologist - Fellow of the American Statistical Association and past AAPOR president.
  • Champion of survey transparency - a national leader in open, accountable survey practice.
  • Director, Survey Research Laboratory (UIC), 1996–2019 - and steward of nearly $30M in research.
